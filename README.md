## About Me

- 🔋 Mechatronics engineering graduate working on **battery repurposing and second-life energy storage systems**
- ⚡ Focused on **power electronics, PCB design, and embedded firmware** — inverters, MPPT charge controllers, and multi-actuator subsystem controllers
- 🏆 Competing in the **Kilikawi Challenge** with **Team RETROFIT**, designing a 2 kW / 48 V full-bridge inverter paired with a 48 V / 25 A MPPT charge controller
- 🚗 Contributing to **e-mobility market research** on battery energy storage ecosystems with AceleAfrica
- 🎓 Currently pursuing graduate study opportunities in **power electronics / chip design**
- 🌱 Interested in renewable energy systems, EV infrastructure, and grid-scale storage

## 🔧 Featured Projects

| Project | Description | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| **Kilikawi Inverter** | 2 kW / 48 V single-stage full-bridge inverter (H-bridge → transformer → AC filter), 230 V / 50 Hz output, aligned with TI's LF inverter reference design | UCC27211A gate drivers, IRF100P219 MOSFETs, SPWM |
| **MPPT Charge Controller** | 48 V / 25 A (1200 W) MPPT solar charge controller redesigned from the LibreSolar 2420HC topology for a LiFePO₄ pack | IRF100P219 MOSFETs, UCC27211 drivers, P&O MPPT |
